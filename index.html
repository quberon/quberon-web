Evidence-first robotics safety

Intervention systems that show their evidence.

Quberon is building a manufacturer-neutral intervention and evidence layer for robots: a way to request STOP, retain the latch, record what happened, and let reviewers verify the result.

Approach

STOP stays available

If optional attestation is unavailable, an authenticated STOP still proceeds and records that limitation instead of pretending certainty.

Permission stays fail-closed

Reset, task execution and other motion-permitting actions require trusted authorization. Rejection and ambiguity do not become motion.

Claims follow evidence

Simulation is labelled simulation. Unimplemented capabilities stay visibly unimplemented. Reviewers get artefacts, not adjectives.

Current status

  • Proven in simulation: STOP priority, latch behaviour, replay and conformance evidence.
  • On hardware: Raspberry Pi camera and simulated robot control demonstrations.
  • Not claimed: physical stopping performance, certification, or manufacturer support.

The detailed evidence lives in quberon/quberon-core.
